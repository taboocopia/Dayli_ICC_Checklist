ICC Trade Checklist

Run through this before every single trade. No shortcuts.

What This Is

This checklist walks you through every step of the ICC framework in the exact order you need to execute it, from confirming your HTF bias, to identifying your Pre-Indication Level, all the way through entry, stop loss, and take profit. Nothing is skipped. Nothing is assumed.

How To Use It
1

Before you touch a chart open this checklist. Confirm your HTF bias on the 1H and 4H. Mark your Pre-Indication Level. Know your target before you look for a setup.

2

At each phase check each box only when it is fully confirmed with a candle closure. No guessing. No halfway checks. Every box checked = every rule followed.

3

The only unchecked box is the one that does not match your trend direction. Bullish setup? The bearish confirmation box stays empty. That's not a problem. But other than that, every other box should be checked.

The Golden Rule Candle Closure Confirmation
Every phase of ICC must be confirmed with a candle closure through the Pre-Indication Level. A wick does not count. Price must fully close through the level.
Phase 1A 1M candle closes through the Pre-Indication Level. This confirms the Indication.
Phase 2Price pulls back and retests the Pre-Indication Level. The retest confirms this is a valid ICC setup.
Phase 3A 1M candle closes back through the level in the direction of the trend. This is your entry trigger.
No candle closure = no confirmation = no trade. Every time.
Full ICC Flow — Lock This In
HTF: Bias + Pre-Indication Level → 1M Close: Indication → Watch: Correction → 2nd Close: Confirmation → Below HL: Stop Loss → Limit: Entry → 1:1 / 1:2: TP + TP2
Walk away if any of the yes/no questions are RED.

HTF Bias & Setup Conditions
1H 4H
Check your bias
Are we in an uptrend or downtrend? Higher highs and higher lows (bullish) or lower highs and lower lows (bearish)?
Check
Is there clear 1H or 4H bias?
If bias isn't clear, don't force a setup. Wait for clarity.
4H
Identify your swing high and swing low levels
Mark the key swing highs and swing lows on the 4H timeframe. These are your structural reference points.
1H
Mark your support and resistance levels
Identify key support and resistance on the 1H timeframe. Where has price reacted before?
1H 4H
Identify the most recent swing highs and swing lows
These can be marked on both 1H and 4H. These are the levels you are actively watching for a reaction.
Key Level
Mark your Pre-Indication Level
The swing level that price has NOT broken or retested yet. This is the level you are watching for your indication. Mark it in advance.
Target
Identify where price is likely headed
Previous highs, equal highs, or liquidity pools above.
2
Phase 1 · ICC
Indication
Drop to 1M
1M
A 1M candle CLOSES past the Pre-Indication Level
Body close only. A wick through does NOT count. Must be a full candle close.
Bullish
Candle closes ABOVE swing high
The Pre-Indication Level is now the Defended Swing. Direction is confirmed up.
Bearish
Candle closes BELOW swing low
The Pre-Indication Level is now the Defended Swing. Direction is confirmed down.
Do NOT enter here. Wait for the correction. The indication is just confirmation of direction.
3
Phase 2 · ICC
Correction
Watch. Don't touch.
Do Nothing
Price pulls back against the trend
This is normal. Do nothing. The market is refueling (the private jet is landing).
1M
Observe the pullback
Price moves against the trend back through the Pre-Indication Level. Bullish: sellers temporarily step in. Let it happen.
LTF Watch
Watch for internal structure shift
The correction is losing steam and price is starting to shift back in the direction of the trend. This is your signal that the pullback may be ending.
Invalidation
Did structure break the WRONG way?
If price breaks below a key swing (bullish setup) = correction failed. Walk away. Wait for new setup.
Correction capped at 30 points.
Check
Did you wait for the retest before entering?
The correction is a watch-only phase. Entering during the pullback, before the retest and close, is not a valid entry.
The correction is not complete at the pullback. It is complete at the RETEST + CLOSE.
4
Phase 3 · ICC
Continuation Entry
Execute with precision
1M Close
Candle CLOSES back through the swing level
Bullish: close back ABOVE the level. Bearish: close back BELOW. Correction is complete. Trend wants to continue.
Check
Did the candle close through the level (not just the wick)?
Body close only. A wick through the level does not confirm the entry.
Check
Do you feel calm and clear before entering?
If you feel rushed or uncertain, that's a sign to slow down, not a reason to enter.
Limit Order
Place Buy Limit at the defended swing level
After the confirmation close, limit order at the defended swing level. I let price come to me.
🚌 Bus Pass

Used when price doesn't pull all the way back to retest the swing but gets within 5 points of the swing. This gives you a 5-point buffer for entry instead of waiting for a full retest.

No Fill
If limit is not hit and price leaves: no trade
Did this happen?
Do not market order after it. Mark it as a missed trade and move on. Another setup will come.
Stop Loss
Set stop loss immediately
Bullish: below the higher low of the correction. Bearish: above the lower high. This is your invalidation level.
Take Profit
Set TP1 at 1:1 (partials) + TP2 at structure
Target liquidity pools, previous highs/lows, or external structure. I shoot for 1:2 minimum on MNQ.
TP1 Hit
Take partials + move SL to breakeven
Let the rest run to structure. Do not close the whole trade out of fear at TP1.
In Trade
Let it play out. Do not interfere.
No random stop moves. No emotional closes. You already planned this. Trust the structure.
